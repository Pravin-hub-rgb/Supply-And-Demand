# Part B: Theory and Rules Summary

1. **All-Time High / No Supply Zone rule (p43):** If price is at an ATH with no overhead supply and heavy aggressive selling (big red candles), strictly trade only the **conservative zone**.


2. **MTF Fallback (p43):** If Higher TF has a Demand Zone (DZ) but Execution TF (ETF) lacks a matching zone, you can drop/look at Medium TF (MTF) to trade (applicable for 10% cases), though the Stop Loss will be larger.


3. **Higher TF Location Mandatory (p43):** Even for lower timeframe setups (like DSI or WIT), you must check Quarterly, Half-Yearly, and Yearly charts. Without checking higher TF context, a WIT trade can fail if price is dropping straight out of a higher TF (Quarterly/Monthly) supply zone.


4. **Supply Zone Interference before Target (p45):** If a Monthly Supply Zone is active (pending orders picked), a regular Daily Demand Zone cannot stop it—only a **Monthly Demand Zone** (or an extremely strong Weekly Demand Zone that broke an explosive supply zone) can hold.


5. **Aggressive Level / STF breaking HTF Supply (p46):** When an STF demand zone breaks an HTF supply zone, it acts as an **aggressive level** with high momentum (capable of breaking R2 and achieving targets). Avoid execution zones that lack "achievement".
